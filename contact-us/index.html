BEFORE WE BEGIN

A little clarity.

What should I prepare for a consultation?+

A floor plan, reference images and a sense of your priorities are helpful. It is equally fine to start with questions. The first conversation helps shape a brief.

Can we work on just one room?+

Yes. The studio takes on single rooms — a kitchen, bedroom or living room — as well as complete homes and commercial spaces. Tell us the rooms involved in your enquiry and we will confirm availability for your dates.

How are fees and timelines decided?+

Scope, size, level of detail and project conditions all influence the proposal. Fees, deliverables and timelines should be confirmed in writing before work begins.
